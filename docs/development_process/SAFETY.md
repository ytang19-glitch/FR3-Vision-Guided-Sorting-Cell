# Safety Principle

Always develop in this order:

```text
Simple
  ↓
Repeatable
  ↓
Fake hardware
  ↓
Low-speed real hardware
  ↓
Fixed-coordinate grasping
  ↓
Vision
  ↓
Autonomous operation
```

Never test a new motion for the first time at full speed on the real FR3.
